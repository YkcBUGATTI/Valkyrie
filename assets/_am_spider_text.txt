The pursuit of open-top luxury at speed comes with the challenge of remaining true to Aston Martin’s precise performance objectives. The Aston Martin Valkyrie Spider has inherited unique engineering solutions born from the combined talents of Aston Martin and Red Bull Advanced Technologies. That's an enviable starting point.

Engineering and Performance


                        By adhering to the Aston Martin Valkyrie ethos, the Aston Martin Valkyrie Spider retains its world-beating package of efficient and lightweight solutions and layers its own unique personality on top. Just what you'd expect from the closest thing you’ll ever get to a road-legal F1® Aston Martin.

AERODYNAMICS PACKAGE

ULTRA-LIGHTWEIGHT CONSTRUCTION


                                        Key to Aston Martin Valkyrie Spider's spine-tingling performance is its ultra-lightweight construction and high downforce aerodynamics package. But it had to look good too. The upper body surfaces of the teardrop-shaped cockpit and lower tub contours follow the envelope of space between the huge full-length Venturi tunnels that run either side of the cockpit floor. They draw vast quantities of air beneath the car to feed the rear diffuser, generating colossal levels of downforce. While that's crucial to Aston Martin Valkyrie Spider's prodigious performance, the tunnels also keep the upper body surfaces free from additional aerodynamic devices that would spoil the purity of the legendary open top styling. Looks, after all, are everything.
                                    

CARBON FIBRE STRUCTURE

PRECISE RE-CALIBRATION


                                        While the Aston Martin Valkyrie's unique 1155PS Hybrid V12 powertrain stays the same, the Aston Martin Valkyrie Spider has been the lucky recipient of a few key revisions to its carbon fibre structure, as well as precise re-calibration of both the active aerodynamic and active chassis systems. All designed to keep the legendary Aston Martin Valkyrie performance up when the roof is down.
                                    

F1® INSPIRED

EXTREME OPEN-TOP


                                        Aston Martin Valkyrie's renowned driver-focused cabin sits at the heart of Aston Martin Valkyrie Spider.

It's no secret that the cabin has been inspired by F1®, which only adds to the pulse-racing sensation of driving the fastest and most extreme open-top Aston Martin ever built. The cabin has low hip-to-heel height and near-central seat positioning, along with a 6-point safety harness to hold the occupants safely in the car. Displays sit just above the instrument panel and a centre rear-view camera system as well as front and rear parking sensors help the driver at both high and low speeds. Yes, the Aston Martin Valkyrie Spider can be driven at low speeds.



                        The driving experience promises to be an intense experience shaped by the unbeatable sensory stimulus that comes with roof-off driving in one of the world’s most extreme hypercars.

                    


                            Marek Reichman, EVP and CCO
