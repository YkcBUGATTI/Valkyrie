For those with racing in their blood

A new icon. A rare opportunity. An experience like no other.


Introducing the Aston Martin Valkyrie LM, a limited edition, track-focused evolution of the Aston Martin Valkyrie Hypercar, developed directly from our 2025 competitor in the FIA World Endurance Championship (WEC) and IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship (IMSA). Limited to just ten cars worldwide, this is more than ownership. It's a gateway into the soul of endurance racing.


For ten exclusive owners, the Aston Martin Valkyrie LM Experience offers a fully immersive, bespoke driver development programme, placing them at the heart of Aston Martin’s racing legacy. Behind the wheel of their very own V12 naturally aspirated Aston Martin Valkyrie LM, at some of the world’s most iconic circuits. With every aspect of logistics, vehicle transport, and technical support meticulously managed.


Just arrive. Step in. And focus entirely on extracting the very edge of performance.

Aston Martin Valkyrie LM

Revolutionary. Ferociously fast. Race-optimised. Our new limited edition Valkyrie LM is the ultimate incarnation of our Le Mans hypercar and a direct tribute to Aston Martin's storied history and hard-fought triumph. From our first entry at Le Mans in 1928, through multiple class wins across the decades, the Valkyrie LM now marks the triumphant return of our wings to the top echelon of the sport as we bid for outright victory once more.


Near identical to the race car currently competing in WEC and IMSA, with only minimal differentiation, the Valkyrie LM embodies the pinnacle of motorsport engineering. We’ve meticulously fine-tuned the Valkyrie Hypercar to unlock its full potential in the hands of passionate enthusiasts, removing race series specific items such as ballast and FIA-regulation electronics, replaced with a bespoke cockpit interface tailored for track day use puts you in total command. This is racing unleashed, pure and unadulterated.

ENGINEERING & PERFORMANCE

ENGINE

Sporting the same lean-burn version of the sensational Cosworth-built 6.5 litre naturally aspirated V12 engine as our Valkyrie Hypercar, Valkyrie LM is tuned to an identical regulation-limited 520kW (697bhp). An intensive dyno development programme has retuned and remapped every parameter to both comply with hypercar regulations and to arm the Valkyrie LM for the rigors of endurance racing, optimising fuel consumption and drivability.

 

To maximise drivability, Valkyrie LM features open rather than closed-loop torque control, enhancing the driving experience and eliminating the driveshaft and input torque sensors that manage power delivery on the homologated race car. The V12 is recalibrated to accept readily available fuel, and Aston Martin's elite team of professional drivers and engineers will formulate a driver-friendly handling setup, ensuring the Valkyrie LM delivers the ultimate performance on the track.

CHASSIS

The Valkyrie LM's beating heart is its modified chassis, meticulously re-engineered to accommodate an expanded fuel tank, a necessity for the grueling stints of endurance racing. Precise modifications to the bulkhead structure allow for a larger fuel cell to be seamlessly integrated, all without compromising the torsional rigidity or weight distribution of the chassis. The result is a chassis that, while building on the foundations of the Aston Martin Valkyrie AMR Pro, pushes every millimeter to arm the Valkyrie LM for the unique demands of endurance racing.

 

Even the door structure has been transformed, its interaction with the bodywork's top deck evolved in the name of driver safety and efficiency. Ingress and egress have been refined to shave precious seconds off pit stops, where races are won and lost.

SUSPENSION AND TRANSMISSION

The racing suspension configuration features double wishbones front and rear, with pushrod actuated torsion bar springs and adjustable side and central dampers. Wishbone geometries have been modified from Valkyrie AMR Pro to sharpen race vehicle dynamics.

 

An additional anti-penetration member in the front wishbones ups the safety factor. Springs and dampers have been fully reoptimised and precisely tuned to wield ultimate control over the race car's aerodynamic platform. The rear-wheel-drive Valkyrie LM channels its power through an Xtrac seven-speed sequential transmission, with lightning-fast changes delivered by semi-automatic paddle shift.

WHEELS, TYRES AND BRAKES

Brimming with F1® technologies the Aston Martin Valkyrie LM will run on bespoke performance tyres from F1® tyre partner, Pirelli. The wheels have been reengineered, their rim profiles subtly redesigned to accommodate the outboard mechanical components. Freed from the wheel cover requirements, our engineers seized the opportunity to eliminate the wheel cover mounts, shedding every excess gram in an unapologetic display of weight saving.

 

Nestled within these lightened wheels lies a race-specific carbon brake setup, fully inherited from the race car and developed to deliver uncompromising braking performance and endurance under the punishing conditions of the track. The front brake duct has been completely reworked to flood the brake disc with cooling airflow during the heat of competition. This is braking performance ruthlessly focused on shaving every possible millisecond off lap times.

EXTERIOR

EXTERIOR ARTISTRY

The Aston Martin Valkyrie LM's exterior is a masterclass in relentless innovation and uncompromising performance. The striking, cutting-edge exterior showcases the extreme aerodynamics of the Aston Martin Valkyrie Hypercar and exemplifies its track credentials in extraordinary detail. Every crafted line, every curve of its pioneering profile embodies our relentless focus on improving airflow, maximising downforce, and reducing drag.

 

EXCEPTIONAL AERODYNAMICS

The ultra-lightweight, all-carbon fibre bodywork is constructed without compromise to prioritise performance and handling. Valkyrie LM features a new, large rear wing that adds even more ferocious performance and augments the car's exceptional aerodynamics, made possible through space-age engineering. A front end dominated by large air intakes cools the engine and increases airflow, while incredible amounts of air drawn through vast full-length venturi tunnels feed the rear diffuser. Combined with the groundbreaking rear wing, these elements work in harmony to give the car extraordinary levels of grip and downforce, cementing Valkyrie LM’s status as a true hypercar.

 

A BLANK CANVAS FOR YOUR PERSONAL EXPRESSION

Each of the ten Valkyrie LM cars presents a blank canvas for your personal expression. Unlike the vinyl-wrapped race cars, your Valkyrie LM will be hand-painted in the colour of your choice from Aston Martin's extensive palette. This is your chance to claim your piece of motorsport history.

INTERIOR

RAW RACING INTERIOR

Settle into the full race-specification cockpit of your Aston Martin Valkyrie LM, where you'll find a single seat positioned on the left-hand side, tailored for the driver alone. Sink into your custom carbon fibre race seat, designed to hold you in an F1® style driving position, with shoulder support and headrest padding that absorbs G-Forces. Rest your fingers on the manual paddle shift gear change, feeling the six-point FIA 8853 safety harness secure you in place.

 

Grip the ultra-lightweight 6-paddle quick-release electronic carbon fibre power-assisted steering wheel, its integrated driver displays and shift lights keeping you informed and in control. Rotary switches for chassis and engine functions and additional race-specific driver displays surround you, all optimised for track day running.

 

PROFESSIONAL TELEMETERY

Stay in constant communication with your pit crew thanks to the integrated radio communication system, designed specifically for track use and compatible with professional telemetry systems. This setup ensures seamless coordination of strategies and real-time performance updates, crucial for maintaining an advantage during intense track sessions.

 

Monitor every aspect of the Valkyrie LM's performance with the onboard track telemetry system, which captures a wealth of real-time data. Analyse every parameter, from lap times to tyre wear, empowering you to make data-driven decisions and extract every ounce of potential from both car and driver.

The Experience

Beyond exhilaration. Beyond limits. This is an invitation to join the most exclusive high performance community.

 

For ten owners, the Aston Martin Valkyrie LM Experience unlocks the opportunity to push the boundaries of performance and realise their racing potential at the world’s most iconic circuits, in their Valkyrie LM.

 

Embrace the legacy and become an Aston Martin named driver by acquiring a coveted race license, expertly arranged by Aston Martin at the iconic Silverstone circuit in the UK. Then, prepare to explore the intricacies of performance, scrutinising every figure and obsessing over every detail to uncover those elusive marginal gains. Receive detailed driver training and delving deeper into the inner workings of your Valkyrie LM with expert engineers and technicians at each location. And this is just the beginning. Across the 12 months Aston Martin will attend to every detail of your exceptional track experiences ensuring a seamless and unforgettable experience.

 

At the culmination of this extraordinary journey, the choice is yours: request more track days, continue your driver development, or explore new circuits in 2027, extending your legacy on the track.

THE EXPERIENCE

DRIVER PREPARATION

Prepare to dominate the track with Aston Martin's bespoke driver development program - an integral part of the Valkyrie LM experience. World-class instructors, 1:1 coaching, and a relentless pursuit of mastery.

 

This pursuit of perfection extends to every facet of your experience. Your personal performance engineer monitors all your driver inputs, including braking, steering, and lap times, then coaches you on how to improve with the telemetry. At this state-of-the-art centre, every contour of your body is moulded to perfection in a seat unique to you, essential to counteract the Valkyrie LM’s high G-forces. But unparalleled personalisation doesn't end here.

 

TAILORED FOR PEAK PERFORMANCE

Suit up. Your personalised racing ensemble awaits, expertly tailored for peak performance and unparalleled comfort. A custom-fitted suit, shaped by specialists in fireproof FIA-approved material, personalised with your name and the iconic Aston Martin wings badge. Beneath, fireproof underclothes provide an added layer of security. Completing the ensemble: racing boots, gloves, a helmet with moulded earpieces and integrated radio, and a Head and Neck Safety (HANS) device. Every detail, designed for your safety and your success.

THE EXPERIENCE

THE TRACKS

Take exclusive ownership of your Valkyrie LM at a landmark handover event in Q2 2026, the moment where anticipation becomes exhilaration. From that first drive, your journey unfolds with two further bespoke track experiences across Q3 and Q4 2026, held at iconic world-class Formula 1® circuits. Each event is meticulously crafted to elevate your connection with the car and unlock your true potential at the very edge of performance.

OFF TRACK EXPERIENCE

A JOURNEY LIKE NO OTHER

Step into a world where passion meets precision. Connect with fellow Aston Martin enthusiasts, expert technicians and drivers, and unlock the secrets of hypercar performance. An extraordinary opportunity to learn about the intricacies of this incredible hypercar, hone your skills and gain a competitive edge before taking to the track.

 

Prepared to perfection at the circuit, awaiting your command. A full vehicle kit accompanies every Aston Martin Valkyrie LM, equipping you with everything needed to push the boundaries of performance, including a car cover, spare wheel set fitted with wet tyres, high stands, fuel bowsers, a laptop with cables, hub A frames, an on-track timing board, flight cases, and a fitted driver’s seat. To ensure you’re primed to exploit the car’s full potential, collaborate with Aston Martin engineers to craft a bespoke handling setup, optimised to your driving style.

COMPLETE IMMERSION

Sharpen your skills in immersive simulator sessions arranged by Aston Martin at BASE Performance, exploring the Valkyrie LM's limits within a controlled environment. Then, under the guidance of expert instructors, take command of the track. The dedicated Valkyrie LM engineering team will provide in-depth data and onboard video analysis, unlocking the full spectrum of your driving capabilities. Rest assured, your machine will be meticulously stored, maintained, and transported by Aston Martin's specialised team, ensuring seamless performance at every turn.

 

UNFORGETTABLE MOMENTS

Our commitment to exclusive hospitality, accommodation, and chauffeur service extends to every facet of your experience, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in the world of hypercar performance. As a Valkyrie LM owner, you'll be treated to an unrivalled level of service, ensuring that every moment, both on and off the track, is nothing short of extraordinary.

 

From the starting grid to the final checkered flag, your racing journey culminates in a prestigious end-of-season celebration, a fitting conclusion to a season of adrenaline-fueled thrills and unforgettable moments on the world's most iconic circuits.

Valkyrie LM is an exquisite opportunity to be part of the most exclusive Aston Martin owners club in the world; a group who can immerse themselves in the purest and most comparable endurance driving experience, one that would otherwise only be available to our works drivers operating at the very limit of sportscar racing’s highest echelon.
Adrian Hallmark, Aston Martin CEO
