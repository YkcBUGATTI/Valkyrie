# 评测与官方引语笔记 — Aston Martin Valkyrie
> 来源:MotorTrend 首驾评测、Autocar 深度评测、Road & Track 银石纪录报道、媒体库 PR 正文、官网车型页。抓取时间:2026-08-16。

## 一、MotorTrend — "Driven! The $3 Million Aston Martin Valkyrie Is Extreme in the Extreme"(Jonny Lieberman, 2023-03-06)
URL: https://www.motortrend.com/features/2023-aston-martin-valkyrie-first-drive-review(巴林国际赛道试驾)

### 关键数据(评测原文)
- 售价约 **$3,000,000/台**(定制前);150 台 Coupé + 40 台 AMR Pro + 85 台 Spider **全部售罄**。
- 声称干重 2,800 lb(约 1,270 kg),加满油液约 3,000 lb(约 1,360 kg)。
- Cosworth 6.5L V12:**11,100 rpm 红线**,1,001 hp + 575 lb-ft;混动电机另加 141 hp / 207 lb-ft;**综合 1,139 hp @ 10,600 rpm、682 lb-ft @ 7,000 rpm**。
- 下压力:**137–220 mph 区间 2,425 lb 下压力**(接近比 F1 赛车多 1,000 lb);理论可达两吨,但标准轮胎超过 2,500 lb 会爆胎。
- 0-60 mph:2.2 秒(MT 估算);极速 220 mph。
- 尺寸:轴距 109.0 in;长宽高 177.4 x 75.6 x 41.7 in;变速箱 7 速自动离合手动;EPA 油耗约 9/14/11 mpg。

### 要点与原文引语
- 起源:Newey、Horner、Palmer、Sproule 四人聚餐(bangers and mash + "tepid lagers")中谈成项目;最初只计划 15、24、59 台。
- 评价:"As extreme and uncompromised as the automobile is likely to get."(极致且毫不妥协的汽车之最)
- 坦率批评:"Aston neglected to make the Valkyrie fun. There, I said it—I was expecting the car to be more fun."(阿斯顿忘了让它"好玩")
- 结语:"Would I have one, had I the money? In a heartbeat... the astonishing, near-ethereal acceleration is in fact damn fun."

## 二、Autocar — "Tested: 2026 Aston Martin Valkyrie"(深度评测,2026 版页面,正文为 1140bhp 量产车首试)
URL: https://www.autocar.co.uk/car-review/aston-martin/valkyrie(巴林试驾)

### 关键数据
- 标题语:"1140bhp, £2.5m, eight years in the making. We finally take the wheel of Adrian Newey's masterpiece"。
- Cosworth 称发动机设计寿命 **50,000 英里**才需大修。
- 综合下压力可达 1,100 kg(原文 "collective peak of up to 1100kg of aerodynamic assistance")。
- 混动轴向磁通电机位于发动机与变速箱之间,提供低速驱动 —— **无倒挡**。
- 试驾车用 Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2(可选更抓的 Cup 2 R,均合法上路)。
- 高温下 V12 会降转保护(红线动态下调,冷却后恢复)——巴林高温实测。

### 要点与原文引语
- "yes, this is undoubtedly the most extreme factory-built car to legally wear numberplates"(毫无疑问是合法挂车牌的最极致量产车)
- 车内:"even starting it feels special... savagely loud at a 1000rpm idle"、凸轮轴齿轮就在乘员脑后几英寸处。
- 加速:"There is no delay, just instant thrust... close to the immediacy of a high-end EV but continues to grow as revs rise."
- 声浪:"Standing on the pit wall while others blast past proves the V12 sounds amazing from the outside, but sadly not from within."(车外听 V12 极美,车内反而可惜);4000rpm 摘耳机即"ouch",不带防护有听力损伤风险;官方推荐主动降噪耳机。
- 底盘:"the Valkyrie never feels heavy or lacking in agility";滑动过渡"well flagged and easily corrected"。
- 设计协作:首席设计师 Miles Nurnberger 在讨论中要到 8mm 座舱让步,赢得工程团队鼓掌。
- 结语:"Yet the Valkyrie's compromises are all down to the single-minded vision it was born from... the Valkyrie feels like a pinnacle – and also a masterpiece."

## 三、Road & Track — "Aston Martin Valkyrie Smashes Silverstone's Production Car Lap Record"(Emmet White)
URL: https://www.roadandtrack.com/news/a62962689/aston-martin-valkyrie-silverstone-lap-time-record/

### 圈速纪录数据(核心数字)
- **1:56.42 —— 银石 GP 赛道量产车圈速纪录,首个跑进 2 分钟内的民用合法车**。
- 前纪录:Manthey 改装 Porsche 911 GT2 RS,**2:06.82**,被快 **10 秒以上**。
- 对比:Huracan GT3 Evo(光头胎)1:58.165;Formula Renault 2.0 仅以 1:56.052 勉强胜出;F1 赛车 1:27.097。
- 车手:**Darren Turner**(前 F1 测试车手、勒芒冠军);轮胎:Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2;**Hangar 直道约 200 mph**;动力 1,139 hp V12 混动。
- 条件:视频中可见赛道有蒸发水迹,且此前摩托车赛在赛车线留下橡胶 —— 天时更佳可再快。

### 原文引语(Darren Turner)
- "The car performed brilliantly. I'm not sure I had anything more to give, but maybe on a day that was kinder with the conditions we could find a few more tenths if not more."
- "It blows my mind that this is a road-legal car that you can use to drive to Silverstone and then go faster than the pole lap record in the British GT championship."

## 五、评测站综合结论(供网站文案参考)
- 共识:史上最极致、最不妥协的合法公路车;V12 声浪与加速是核心体验;下压力惊人(公路胎为其物理短板);日常性近乎为零但这就是设计本意;驾驶舱窄、进出困难、噪音大。
- 价格锚点:约 $3M(约 £2.5m)。
- 批评点:MT 认为缺乏"乐趣"、Autocar 认为刹车初段脚感略虚、高温降转、无倒挡。
