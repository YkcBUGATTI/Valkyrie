AGGRESSIVE AERODYNAMICS

A WEAPON OF UNPRECEDENTED POTENCY


                                        Taking our partnership with Red Bull Advanced Technologies (RBAT) to stellar heights, the Aston Martin Valkyrie AMR Pro is a weapon of unprecedented potency. A unique version of the Aston Martin Valkyrie chassis gives AMR Pro a 380mm longer wheelbase and a track that is 96mm wider at the front and 115mm wider at the rear. A ferocious aerodynamic package adds an additional 266mm in length and generates extraordinary levels of downflow thanks to engineering mastery of the underbody and overwing airflow. With twice the downflow of the Aston Martin  Valkyrie road car, the Aston Martin Valkyrie AMR Pro attains lateral acceleration of more than 3G.
                                    

BLISTERING PERFORMANCE

INSANELY FAST


                                        Aston Martin Valkyrie AMR Pro relies solely on a modified version of the sensational Cosworth-built 6.5 litre naturally-aspirated V12 engine. Revving to 11,000 rpm, this masterpiece of internal combustion is on target to develop 1000nhp. Insanely fast, by anybody's standards.
                                    

RACE OPTIMISED

LE MANS


                                        First seen at the 2018 Motor Show, the original concept proposed for Le Mans was designed to squeeze in as much performance from the road-legal Aston Martin Valkyrie as possible. The new Aston Martin Valkyrie AMR Pro reaps the benefits of the Le Mans project's race-optimised chassis, aerodynamics and powertrain technology.
                                    



                        Aston Martin Valkyrie AMR Pro is a project beyond compare, a true ‘no rules’ track-only version.

                    


                            Marek Reichman, EVP and CCO
